# Photos needed — MyAfricanDishCompanion

We currently use emoji as stand-ins. Swap them by dropping your files into
`assets/` with the exact filenames below — the app will pick them up
(no data changes needed beyond that).

**Photo spec:** JPG, square-ish, min 800px on the short side.
Finished dishes: plated and ready to eat. Ingredients: raw and recognisable
(what a shopper needs to identify in a market).
